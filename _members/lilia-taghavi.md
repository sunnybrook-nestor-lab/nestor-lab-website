My name is Lilia, and I am a first-year MSc student in the Institute of Medical Science at the University of Toronto under the supervision of Dr. Sean Nestor at Sunnybrook.

My research focuses on treatment-resistant depression, where I analyze blood-based inflammatory and metabolic biomarkers alongside clinical and neuroimaging outcomes from a TMS-fMRI study examining how the brain responds to stimulation in real time and whether these responses are associated with treatment outcomes. I also contribute to an ongoing clinical trial investigating a ketogenic diet alongside personalized, accelerated iTBS and how associated metabolic changes relate to treatment outcomes.

I received my Honours Bachelor of Medical Sciences with a specialization in Physiology and Pharmacology from Western University, where I completed my thesis with Dr. Emily Day studying LONP1 in immunometabolism and later received a UROP award to investigate MAGEA3/A6 in cancer cell survival and treatment response in Dr. Dalia Barsyte-Lovejoy's lab at the University of Toronto with the Structural Genomics Consortium.

Outside of research, I am passionate about community involvement and currently volunteer with the Alzheimer Society of York Region, and I have a longstanding love for teaching, having tutored students in a variety of subjects since high school.
