Dr. Adel Alshehri is a board-certified psychiatrist and clinical fellow in Interventional Neuropsychiatry at the University of Toronto, based at Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre.

Adel is interested in how a deeper understanding of the brain can translate into meaningful improvements in people's lives, particularly when standard psychiatric treatments have not provided enough relief. His clinical and research interests include brain stimulation, treatment-resistant depression, and the thoughtful use of emerging technologies in mental-health care. He is drawn to questions that connect neuroscience with clinical practice: how treatments work, why responses differ, and how care can become more responsive to the individual.

He values collaborative learning and the perspectives that patients and colleagues bring to these questions. Through his training and future work, he hopes to contribute to the development of interventional psychiatry services and research collaborations that make effective, evidence-based care more accessible.

Outside his clinical and academic work, Adel enjoys reading about neuroscience, travelling, and exploring new cities and cultures. He appreciates opportunities to step outside familiar settings, meet people with different experiences, and discover new ways of looking at the world.
