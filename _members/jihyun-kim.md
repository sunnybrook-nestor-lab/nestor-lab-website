Dr. Ji Hyun Kim is a psychiatrist from Busan, South Korea, with more than ten years of clinical experience, including as Chief of Psychiatry at several major hospitals, among them Haeundae Bumin Hospital. She trained in medicine at Dong-A University and in psychiatry at Konkuk University Chungju Hospital, both in Korea, and holds a PhD in clinical psychiatry.

Her clinical interest is in newer treatments for people who have not improved with standard care. Her research asks how the brain changes in response to treatment, and whether markers in blood and brain imaging can predict who will benefit from which treatment.

At Sunnybrook she works with Dr. Sean Nestor's team, seeing patients referred for brain stimulation and other advanced treatments in interventional psychiatry. She plans to bring these approaches back to Korea, where they are still becoming established.
