## ALIGN Study

**Adjunctive Ketogenic Diet + Accelerated iTBS for Treatment-Resistant Depression**

{% include alert.html type="info" content="ALIGN is preparing to open for recruitment. If you are interested, you are welcome to get in touch now, and we will contact you once enrolment begins." %}

We are studying whether a ketogenic diet, combined with a personalized, image-guided form of brain stimulation, can improve symptoms in people whose depression has not responded to antidepressant medication.

Everyone in the study receives **active brain stimulation**. There is no placebo or sham group.

### Who we are looking for

You may be eligible if you:

- Are between **18 and 65** years old
- Are currently experiencing a **major depressive episode**, with at least moderate symptoms
- Have **treatment-resistant depression** — meaning either your depression has not responded to **at least two** adequate courses of antidepressant medication, or you have been unable to tolerate **at least two** antidepressant medications
- Have **not** had rTMS during your current depressive episode (rTMS in a previous episode is fine)
- Are able to commit to a **12-week dietary program** and are willing to follow either diet you are assigned to

You would likely **not** be eligible if you:

- Have a diagnosis of bipolar disorder or a psychotic disorder
- Have a history of epilepsy, seizures, stroke, or another major neurological condition
- Are currently on a ketogenic or low-carbohydrate diet, or have been in the past 6 months
- Have had an eating disorder in the past 12 months, or have a BMI under 20
- Are pregnant, nursing, or planning to become pregnant during the study
- Cannot have an MRI scan
- Are unwilling to do a daily finger-stick blood test

This is not a complete list. Eligibility is confirmed at a screening visit, and not everyone who contacts us will be eligible.

### What participation involves

Participation runs for about **12 months** in total, but the most demanding part is concentrated into a single week.

**Diet — 12 weeks.** You will be randomly assigned to one of two diets and supported throughout by a registered dietitian:

- a **ketogenic diet** — low-carbohydrate and high-fat, with 8 one-hour dietitian sessions, or
- a **Canadian Food Guide diet** — general healthy-eating guidelines, with 5 one-hour dietitian sessions.

You will do a finger-stick test each morning to measure ketones and/or glucose, and keep a record of what you eat using a method you agree on with your dietitian.

**Brain imaging.** A one-hour MRI scan about three weeks before treatment begins, used to locate a personalized stimulation target in your brain. A second MRI four weeks after treatment.

**Treatment week.** Five consecutive days at Sunnybrook. Each day you arrive around 9:00 a.m. and receive 8 stimulation sessions, each about 9 minutes long, with a 50-minute break between sessions. **Expect to be at the hospital roughly 7.5 hours per treatment day.**

**Assessments.** Blood samples, questionnaires about mood, sleep and daily functioning, and physical measures at several points, plus brief online follow-up questionnaires at 6 and 12 months.

### Things you should know

- All in-person visits take place at **Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre**.
- **Participants are not paid** for taking part in this study.
- **Parking is not reimbursed** (up to $30/day at Sunnybrook).
- If you are assigned to the ketogenic diet, your **grocery costs may be higher**, and food costs are not reimbursed.
- Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time, and declining to participate will not affect your access to rTMS through the Harquail Centre's regular clinical pathway.
