Dr. Christina Nona completed her PhD in the Department of Pharmacology and Toxicology at the University of Toronto, where she investigated neuroplastic adaptations within the brain's reward circuitry associated with chronic alcohol exposure. Using rodent models, her research examined the neuromodulatory mechanisms underlying addictive and relapse-like behaviours, contributing to our understanding of individual differences in vulnerability to alcohol use disorder.

She subsequently completed a Postdoctoral Fellowship at the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health (CAMH), where her work focused on translating neurobiological insights from preclinical models into clinical research aimed at improving the treatment of alcohol use disorder.

Dr. Nona's current research focuses on identifying biomarkers of response to repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) in individuals with treatment-resistant depression. Her research has been recognized through numerous prestigious awards and fellowships, including the NSERC Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship, NSERC André Hamer Postgraduate Prize, NSERC Julie Payette Research Scholarship, a CAMH Postdoctoral Fellowship, and most recently support through the Labatt Family Network for Research on the Biology of Depression.
